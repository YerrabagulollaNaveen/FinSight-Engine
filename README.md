# FinSight-Engine
It turns complex data into a clear, actionable insight. Instead of spending hours analyzing charts and numbers, FinSight Engine does the heavy lifting, giving you an AI-powered second opinion to help you make smarter, data-driven decisions in the market.

What is it?

FinSight Engine is a smart tool designed to help investors and traders predict future market trends. You simply upload a file with historical market data (like stock prices in a spreadsheet), and our AI analyzes it to forecast whether the market is likely to move up, down, or sideways.

How does it work in three simple steps?

Upload Data: You can upload your market data in any common format you use, including Excel (.xls, .xlsb) or CSV files.

AI Analysis: The "engine" automatically calculates important financial indicators from your data, just like a professional analyst would. It then uses a trained Machine Learning model to find hidden patterns in these indicators.

Get Insight: Based on its analysis, FinSight Engine gives you a straightforward prediction of the future market trend and shows you interactive charts to help you visualize the data.
